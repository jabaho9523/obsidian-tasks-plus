**Tasks from your meeting notes**

[Meetings Plus](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/meetings-plus) 0.8 can turn `T:` lines in your meeting notes into tasks. Tasks Plus lists them with everything else, so a task from Tuesday's meeting shows up in Today or Anytime, and clicking its note name opens the meeting. Check it off in either place.

**What's new window**

After an update to a new version, Tasks Plus now shows what changed, like this window. Turn it off in the settings, or open it any time with the command **Show what's new**. The notes come with the plugin, so the window makes no network calls.
